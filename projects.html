Selected Research Projects

Current and recent projects connecting physiological measurements, machine learning, and perioperative medicine.

Wearables · Perioperative Medicine

Preoperative Cardiorespiratory Fitness from Wearable Data

Developed an interpretable framework for estimating preoperative cardiorespiratory fitness from free-living wearable measurements, with emphasis on clinically understandable predictors and practical screening.

Read the publication →

ECG · Deep Learning · Phenotyping

ECG-Based Physiological and Echocardiographic Phenotyping

Developing interpretable ECG-based modeling workflows to characterize cardiovascular phenotypes, evaluate transportability across clinical domains, and study associations with postoperative outcomes.

Perioperative Outcomes · Clinical ML

Postoperative Outcome and Length-of-Stay Prediction

Machine-learning analysis of perioperative clinical data, laboratory measurements, vital signs, medications, diagnoses, and intraoperative physiology to predict postoperative recovery and length of stay.

Physiological Data Infrastructure

Vital Sign Registry for Digital Health Research

Development of a perioperative vital-sign registry to support physiological signal research, algorithm development, reproducible analysis, and future clinical digital-health studies.

EEG · Explainable AI

Neurological State Prediction from EEG

Explainable machine-learning studies using EEG for driving-state prediction, stroke-related neurological biomarkers, tinnitus prediction, and other physiological state-estimation problems.
